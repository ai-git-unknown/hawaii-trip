Go counterclockwiseEast coast first keeps you away from Honolulu morning traffic. Food trucks on the North Shore are on the ocean side of the highway going this direction.
⏰
Start early — 6:00 AM is idealOahu's H-1 freeway rivals mainland rush hours. By 6 AM it's clear. The North Shore two-lane road also gets congested on weekends from mid-morning.
⛽
Fill up before leaving WaikikiGas stations are sparse along the east coast and North Shore. Fill the tank at the start.
⚠
The road doesn't fully circle the islandThe highway dead-ends at Ka'ena Point on the northwest tip. This route cuts back inland via H-2 — the standard way to complete the loop.
🚗
Best car for this driveA Jeep Wrangler or convertible for the coastal sections. Book your rental early — demand is high and prices spike close to your travel date.
